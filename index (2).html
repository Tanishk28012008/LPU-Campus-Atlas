Unfolding your campus…Buildings, landmarks & walking paths

Your campus, unfolded.

Campus Atlas is an independent student map of Lovely Professional University, Phagwara.

Walking directions

Routes follow connected outdoor paths in OpenStreetMap and end on the mapped path beside a building. Times assume a walking pace of 75 metres per minute. Indoor access, live closures, accessibility and every building name still need campus verification.

The 3D view

Building outlines come from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps. Heights use mapped levels where available. Other heights, façades and landscaping are illustrative. Blocks inside a connected complex use separate searchable pins.

Use the map

Drag to explore, scroll or pinch to zoom, and Shift-drag or use the rotate button to turn the model. Pick a building or tap anywhere inside campus to set a start or destination. Saved places stay on this device.
